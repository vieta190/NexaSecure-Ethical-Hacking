# Day 1: Cybersecurity Introduction & Ethics

## Topics Covered
* **CIA Triad:** Confidentiality (access control), Integrity (hashing), Availability (redundancy).
* **Ethical vs. Malicious Hacking:** Authorized testing with explicit RoE vs. unauthorized exploitation.
* **Legal Frameworks & Scope:** All testing is strictly restricted to local lab VMs (CFAA compliance).
